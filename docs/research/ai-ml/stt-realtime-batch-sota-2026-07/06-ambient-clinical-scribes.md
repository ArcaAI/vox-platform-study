# Ambient Clinical Documentation Systems — Architecture Reference (mid-2026)

Scope note: Almost all of these are proprietary SaaS. Their ASR/LLM stacks are **not open**, so much below is what vendors *say* on engineering blogs/press (self-reported, often marketing-adjacent) plus a few independent academic evaluations. I mark **[PUBLIC]** = vendor-stated or reported, **[INDEP]** = third-party study, **[INFERRED]** = common-practice pattern not confirmed for that specific vendor.

## 1. Microsoft / Nuance — DAX Copilot → Dragon Copilot

- **[PUBLIC]** On **2025-03-03** Microsoft merged **DAX Copilot** (ambient) + **Dragon Medical One** (dictation) into one product, **Dragon Copilot** — "the healthcare industry's first unified voice AI assistant." Runs on Azure; GA in US/Canada in May 2025, then UK/Germany/France/Netherlands. ([news.microsoft.com, 2025-03-03](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2025/03/03/microsoft-dragon-copilot-provides-the-healthcare-industrys-first-unified-voice-ai-assistant-that-enables-clinicians-to-streamline-clinical-documentation-surface-information-and-automate-task/); [fiercehealthcare.com](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/microsoft-debuts-dragon-copilot-ai-clinical-assistant-nurses-expands-access))
- **Streaming vs. batch / real-time preview:** **[PUBLIC]** DAX Copilot "listens securely and intelligently **drafts your note in the background**" during the visit, then "generates the draft patient note **in seconds** for the provider's review." So: live capture, near-real-time draft, **mandatory clinician review** — *not* autonomous. ([microsoft.com/industry blog, 2024-08-08](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/blog/healthcare/2024/08/08/dax-copilot-new-customization-options-and-ai-capabilities-for-even-greater-productivity/); [uvahealth.com FAQ](https://www.uvahealth.com/patients-and-visitors/support/ai-note-taking-faq))
  - Architecture-relevant history: the **original DAX** (pre-Copilot, ~2020–2022) used a **human medical-scribe review loop** with hours-long turnaround; **DAX Copilot** (Oct 2023) replaced that with GenAI for seconds-latency drafts. The human-in-the-loop moved from *vendor scribe* → *the clinician*.
- **Models:** **[PUBLIC]** "Combines proven AI and LLMs," Dragon ASR + DAX ambient + "fine-tuned generative AI and healthcare-adapted safeguards." Training scale claimed: **1B+ minutes of medical dictation/year**, **15M+ ambient encounters**, 200+ AI researchers. Specific model names, sizes, latency = **not public**. ([news.microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2025/03/03/microsoft-dragon-copilot-provides-the-healthcare-industrys-first-unified-voice-ai-assistant-that-enables-clinicians-to-streamline-clinical-documentation-surface-information-and-automate-task/))
- **On-device vs. cloud:** **[PUBLIC]** Cloud (Azure) with regional data residency, HITRUST CSF + SOC 2 Type 2. Capture via PowerMic Mobile (iOS); no on-device inference claimed.
- **Whether it re-transcribes with a bigger model post-visit: not public / [INFERRED].**

## 2. Abridge

- **Own ASR (not Whisper-dependent):** **[PUBLIC, self-reported]** As of Aug 2025 their ASR beats Google Medical Conversations and **OpenAI Whisper v3** by **16% relative WER**, **45% fewer errors on new medication names**, Spanish **3.2% WER**, benchmarked on **10K hours** of clinical conversations against "challenge datasets" (new drugs, multi-speaker). Speech heritage: co-founder Florian Metze came from **CMU's Language Technologies Institute**. ([research.contrary.com](https://research.contrary.com/company/abridge))
- **Streaming + real-time note gen:** **[PUBLIC]** Notes/summaries "start to appear **as the conversation progresses**"; clinicians spend **~20 seconds** editing the real-time-generated SOAP note. ([abridge.com/product](https://www.abridge.com/product); [research.contrary.com](https://research.contrary.com/company/abridge))
- **Contextual Reasoning Engine (April 2025):** **[PUBLIC]** "an orchestrated system of AI models" that ingests prior notes, health-system guidelines, and clinician preferences, aligns language to **billing/ICD codes**, all "in real time." **Proprietary models rather than a single third-party LLM**, plus "judicious use of cutting-edge LLMs." ([businesswire, 2025-02-17](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250217233814/en/Abridge-Announces-$250M-Series-D-Investment-and-New-Contextual-Reasoning-Engine-to-Streamline-Clinical-and-Financial-Workflows-at-the-Point-of-Care); [abridge.com/ai](https://www.abridge.com/ai))
- **Linked Evidence:** **[PUBLIC]** Every generated note span is mapped back to the exact transcript segment + audio; clinicians click a note line and hear/read the source. This is provenance-for-trust, not just UX. ([abridge.com/ai](https://www.abridge.com/ai))
- **Confabulation elimination (whitepaper, 2025-08-19; Oberst, Liang, Lipton):** **[PUBLIC]** A **separate proprietary detection model** trained on **50,000+ examples** (open-source hallucination datasets + domain clinical data) scores "the level of support of each claim in the note" against transcript + EHR context and emits reasoning; a **correction system** then corrects/deletes/dismisses. It runs on the **"first draft" → "final draft" before the clinician sees it**. Catches **97%** of confabulations vs **82%** for GPT-4o on their 10,000-encounter benchmark. ([abridge.com/ai/science-confabulation-hallucination-elimination, 2025-08-19](https://www.abridge.com/ai/science-confabulation-hallucination-elimination))
- **Scale/funding:** **[PUBLIC]** 150+ health systems, ~50M conversations/yr, 55 specialties, 28 languages; **$757.5M** raised, **$5.3B** valuation (Series E, June 2025). Proprietary training corpus: **1.5M consented de-identified encounters**. ([research.contrary.com](https://research.contrary.com/company/abridge))
- **Diarization:** handles cross-talk/multiple speakers but **implementation not detailed** publicly.

## 3. Nabla (Nabla Copilot)

- **ASR = proprietary Whisper derivative:** **[PUBLIC]** Built on Whisper over "**3 years and $5M**" using **7,000 hours** of manually-annotated medical audio, with custom "improvements to **suppress hallucinations**" and improve medical-term spelling. (Earlier reporting also cited a **mix of fine-tuned Whisper + Microsoft's STT API** — likely legacy/hybrid.) ([nabla.com/blog/how-nabla-uses-whisper](https://www.nabla.com/blog/how-nabla-uses-whisper); [venturebeat.com](https://venturebeat.com/ai/nabla-the-ai-copilot-for-doctors-nets-24m-to-transform-medical-consultations))
- **Verification pipeline (directly relevant to a self-hosted builder):** **[PUBLIC]** transcription → a second layer **extracts facts** → each note is split into **atomic facts**, each **checked via an LLM query against the transcript + patient context** → "**only facts for which we find definitive proof are considered valid.**" Same convergent pattern as Abridge's verifier. ([nabla.com/blog/how-nabla-uses-whisper](https://www.nabla.com/blog/how-nabla-uses-whisper))
- **Streaming, cloud, zero-retention:** **[PUBLIC]** Cloud-first; audio streamed via **WebSocket** in real time and transcribed live; on "Stop encounter" the transcript is processed into a structured note. **Audio discarded by default after note generation** (zero-retention); transcripts/notes kept ~14 days by default. **PII masked before the LLM, unmasked after.** They **explicitly chose cloud + immediate-destroy over on-device**. ([nabla.com privacy blog](https://www.nabla.com/blog/all-you-need-to-know-about-nablas-privacy-and-security-features); [help.nabla.com](https://help.nabla.com/en/articles/768386))
- **Public Core API:** **[PUBLIC]** `/transcribe` WebSocket (real-time) + async; note templates SOAP/APSO/WCC. ([docs.nabla.com](https://docs.nabla.com/2026-04-24/guides/intro))
- **Scale/funding:** **[PUBLIC]** 9M encounters over 24 months (blog); **85,000 clinicians / 130+ orgs**; **$70M Series C (June 2025), $120M total**. ([prnewswire, 2025-06-17](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nabla-raises-70m-series-c-to-deliver-agentic-ai-to-the-heart-of-clinical-workflows-bringing-total-funding-to-120m-302483646.html))

## 4. Suki (Suki Assistant / Suki Platform)

- **Dual-ASR architecture** (their key public design point): **[PUBLIC]** **one ASR for dictation, a second for commands**, with **wakeword-based audio routing + time segmentation** deciding whether audio is a command or dictation. A **syntactic processor** thread handles dictation; a **semantic processor** thread continuously listens for the wake word "Suki" and handles commands. This is the "speed vs. accuracy" split done at the ASR-routing layer. ([suki.ai/blog engineering post](https://www.suki.ai/blog/engineering-an-invisible-and-assistive-voice-agent-for-clinicians/))
- **Medical-tuned ASR + context biasing:** **[PUBLIC]** combines a medically-tuned ASR with context (patient age, physician specialty, appointment type) to disambiguate look-alike terms (hyper- vs hypo-glycemia). Ambient note composition uses **LLMs**; "**no PHI used for summarization; all data stays within Suki.**"
- **Infra:** **[PUBLIC]** **Google Cloud Platform**; backend in **Go + Python**; in-memory caching of patient data/transcript queues/shared state for low latency.
- **Suki Platform = ASR/ambient-as-a-service:** **[PUBLIC]** exposes ambient documentation, speech recognition, and form-filling via APIs to embed in third-party apps (Epic, Cerner/Oracle, Athena, Elation). ([developer.suki.ai](https://developer.suki.ai/documentation/overview); [suki.ai/suki-platform](https://www.suki.ai/suki-platform/))
- **Funding:** **[PUBLIC]** ~**$168M** total (**$70M Series D, Oct 2024**; Zoom Ventures, Jan 2025); KLAS 93.2. ([forbes.com, 2025-05-01](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sindhyavalloppillil/2025/05/01/suki-ai-is-healthcares-new-infrastructure/))
- **[INFERRED / unconfirmed]** Historical reliance on a third-party ASR (Deepgram/Amazon have been speculated) before building in-house — **I could not confirm this publicly; treat as unverified.**

## 5. Corti (EU / Denmark)

Most architecturally transparent of the group — closest reference for a self-hosted builder.

- **Corti Symphony** = "model network and orchestration layer for Text and Audio." **Three STT endpoints:** **[PUBLIC]**
  - `/transcribe` — stateless **WebSocket** streaming for low-latency dictation/voice UI;
  - `/streams` — **WebSocket** for conversational ambient audio tied to a session, with **native speaker identification (diarization)**;
  - `/transcripts` — **REST** for pre-recorded/async batch.
  Delivers **interim (word-by-word) + final** transcripts. ([docs.corti.ai](https://docs.corti.ai/about/introduction); [corti.ai/speech-to-text](https://www.corti.ai/speech-to-text))
- **Explicit architecture philosophy (very useful):** **[PUBLIC]** They critique **Whisper-style AED (attention encoder-decoder)** models as **batch-oriented and prone to over-generation/hallucination**, and favor **streaming CTC/Transducer** models because the metric that matters for live UI is **word-level latency** ("how fast does the word they just said appear"). They also flag **decoder-only ASR** with real-time custom-vocabulary prompting as a promising direction. ([corti.ai/stories/building-better-speech-recognition, 2025-07-22](https://www.corti.ai/stories/building-better-speech-recognition-for-healthcare))
- **Orchestrated (not single-model) pipeline:** **[PUBLIC]** acoustic model → **LM refinement** → **punctuation/formatting** → **domain-adaptation layer** (acronym expansion, unit/notation normalization) → optional structured (JSON/SOAP) post-processing. Developers can **bias recognition toward custom vocabularies** (keyterm biasing → "50% reduction in missed terms"). Uses **synthetic TTS-generated paired data** to teach rare medical vocabulary cheaply. ([corti.ai/stories/why-voice-first-healthcare-ai-needs-medical-grade-asr-pipelines, 2025-10-23](https://www.corti.ai/stories/why-voice-first-healthcare-ai-needs-medical-grade-asr-pipelines))
- **Self-reported accuracy:** **1.4% WER (98.6% word accuracy) English real-time**, 98.5% formatted-entity recall on dosages/units. ([corti.ai/speech-to-text](https://www.corti.ai/speech-to-text))
- **EU sovereign angle:** **[PUBLIC]** Containerized/sovereign-ready deployment, **BSI C5** (Germany), on-EU-soil data residency; powers **Dedalus ORBIS Speech Assistant** for **47,000+ German physicians**, Voicepoint (Switzerland). Company-wide: ~100M patients/yr, 250,000 interactions/day. ([corti.ai/news sovereign](https://www.corti.ai/news/corti-pioneers-europes-first-sovereign-healthcare-ai-infrastructure))
- **Funding:** **[PUBLIC]** **$60M Series B (2023-09), ~$260M valuation.** ([techcrunch.com, 2023-09-20](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/20/corti-an-ai-co-pilot-for-healthcare-clinicians-raises-60m/))

## 6. Cross-cutting patterns (the design-reference core)

**Q: Streaming ASR for live display, then re-transcribe full audio with a bigger/offline model for the final note?**
- **[INDEP / general practice — CONFIRMED as an industry norm, not per-incumbent]** ASR vendors state plainly: "**most production systems run a fast streaming pass for live feedback and a more accurate reprocessing pass once the encounter ends.**" ([assemblyai.com](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/how-to-build-ai-medical-scribe))
- **What's actually confirmed for the incumbents:** all run a **fast live pass** and then a **heavier post-visit pass**, but the heavy pass is publicly described as **LLM note-generation + verification**, *not explicitly a second full ASR re-transcription with a larger model*. Nabla streams live, then "on Stop" processes the transcript into the note. DAX drafts "in the background," finalized in seconds. **Corti's stated philosophy actually argues against a batch Whisper re-scoring** — they push streaming transducers end-to-end. **So: "live streaming + heavier post-visit note pass" is confirmed; "re-transcribe the audio with a bigger offline ASR" is [INFERRED] and plausible but not vendor-confirmed for these five.** Flag accordingly.

**Q: LLM cleanup / punctuation / speaker-role pass before summarization?** — **[PUBLIC] Yes, universally.**
- Punctuation/formatting/casing/units and domain normalization are explicit pipeline stages (Corti's stages 3–4; the "restore readability" step). ([corti.ai](https://www.corti.ai/stories/why-voice-first-healthcare-ai-needs-medical-grade-asr-pipelines))
- A **post-generation verification/fact-check pass before the clinician sees the draft** is a strong convergent pattern: **Abridge** (separate detection model + correction, 97% catch), **Nabla** (atomic-fact LLM verification, "only proven facts kept"). This is the single most-copied non-obvious idea.

**Q: Diarization (clinician vs. patient)?**
- **[PUBLIC]** Corti has **native speaker ID** on its `/streams` endpoint. Others (Abridge, DAX, Nabla) advertise multi-speaker/cross-talk robustness but **don't publish their diarization method.**
- **[INDEP]** Clinical diarization is genuinely hard: 3–4 concurrent speakers, sentence-completion overlap, room reverb; standard broadcast/call-center diarizers fail. The most-cited practical fix is **physician voice enrollment** (a 30-sec reference embedding) plus **pyannote.audio**-style speaker-segmented timestamps merged into ASR output before the LLM — the LLM needs "who said what" because patient-reported symptoms ≠ clinician observations. ([peerbits.com](https://www.peerbits.com/blog/real-time-medical-transcription-challenges.html); [assemblyai.com](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/how-to-build-ai-medical-scribe))

**Q: Accuracy / WER / independent evaluations?**
- **Self-reported:** Corti 1.4% WER (EN real-time); Abridge 16% better than Whisper v3. Treat as vendor benchmarks.
- **[INDEP] NEJM AI RCT (UCLA, published 2025)** — 238 physicians, 14 specialties, DAX Copilot vs Nabla vs control, Nov 2024–Jan 2025: **Nabla −9.5% time-in-note (significant, ~23 s/visit); DAX ~5 s/visit (not significant).** Both showed burnout/task-load improvement; both had "occasional inaccuracies requiring ongoing vigilance." ([ai.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/AIoa2501000](https://ai.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/AIoa2501000); [PMC12265753](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12265753/))
- **[INDEP] "Quality and Safety of Ambient Digital Scribe Platforms Using Simulated Encounters"** (Mayo Clinic Proc: Digital Health / OHSU + MedStar, Oct 2025) — 5 platforms, 14 simulated encounters: transcripts averaged **13.9 errors**, **19.5% of transcript errors propagated into the note**, and **mean note error rate 26.3%** (errors classified omission/commission/partial, harm-scored via AHRQ). This is the most sobering independent data point — **the transcript→note step both filters and introduces errors.** ([mcpdigitalhealth.org](https://www.mcpdigitalhealth.org/article/S2949-7612(25)00099-9/fulltext); [PMC12605248](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12605248/))
- **[INDEP]** JAMA/health-system reporting: ambient scribes cut burnout ~21% across two systems; JMIR time-motion and other 2025–2026 studies broadly positive on documentation burden, mixed on raw throughput ("not yet productivity tools"). ([digitalhealthwire.com](https://digitalhealthwire.com/ai-scribes-arent-productivity-tools-yet/))

## Architecture patterns a self-hosted clinical STT builder should borrow

1. **Two-tier ASR: streaming for live UI, heavier pass for the record.** Optimize the live pass for **word-level latency** with a **streaming CTC/Transducer** (Corti's explicit stance) rather than a batch AED like vanilla Whisper — AED autoregression over-generates and hallucinates on silence/cross-talk. Reserve any larger/slower model (or a full-file re-decode) for the post-"stop" pass that feeds note generation. (Your repo's browser-Whisper + backend-streaming split via `pipelineId` already mirrors tier-1; the tier-2 heavy pass is where to invest.)

2. **Put a verification/fact-check model between draft and clinician — this is the industry's most-copied non-obvious move.** Both Abridge (separate 50K-example detection model + correction) and Nabla (atomic-fact LLM check "only proven facts kept") gate the note on **claim-level support against the transcript + prior context** *before* the human sees it. This maps cleanly onto a guardrail/NLP-verification stage in a summarization pipeline.

3. **Keep provenance: map every generated note span back to its transcript+audio segment (Abridge "Linked Evidence").** It's the trust mechanism that makes clinician review fast and defensible, and it's cheap to build if you retain char-offset alignment from ASR through summarization.

4. **Treat the ASR pipeline as an orchestrated chain, not one model:** acoustic → LM/context refinement → **punctuation/formatting/units/casing** → **domain normalization (acronym/notation)** → structured (SOAP/JSON). Inject a **custom medical vocabulary / keyterm biasing** layer (Corti reports ~50% fewer missed terms), and consider **synthetic TTS-generated audio** to teach rare drug/term spellings without collecting real audio.

5. **Solve diarization with physician voice enrollment + explicit speaker-role tagging feeding the LLM.** Off-the-shelf diarizers fail in exam-room acoustics; a 30-second clinician enrollment embedding plus speaker-segmented timestamps merged into the transcript gives the summarizer the "who said what" it needs (patient-reported vs. clinician-asserted).

6. **Privacy posture is an architecture choice, and cloud+zero-retention is a viable alternative to on-device.** Nabla's model — stream in cloud, **PII-mask before the LLM and unmask after**, **discard audio on note completion**, keep transcripts on a short TTL — is a self-hostable pattern that sidesteps on-device constraints while staying defensible. (For EU/sovereignty, Corti's containerized on-soil deployment + BSI C5 is the reference.) **Independent studies say ~1-in-4 note segments still carry errors, so mandatory clinician review + easy edit-and-correct is non-negotiable regardless of stack.**

**Explicitly unverifiable / flagged:** exact model names, sizes, and latencies for DAX/Dragon Copilot, Abridge, and Nabla (all proprietary); whether any incumbent literally re-runs a *larger ASR* over the full audio post-visit (inferred, not confirmed); Suki's pre-in-house ASR vendor (unconfirmed); all vendor WER figures are self-reported benchmarks, not independently reproduced. The only independent accuracy/safety numbers are the NEJM AI RCT (time-in-note) and the Mayo/OHSU simulated-encounter study (error rates).
