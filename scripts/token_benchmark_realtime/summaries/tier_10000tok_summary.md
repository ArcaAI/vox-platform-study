## Plan of Care
Medications Prescribed: Aspirin 300mg chewed immediately. Loading dose of Ticagrelor 180mg orally. Therapeutic anticoagulation with Enoxaparin 1 mg/kg every 12 hours subcutaneously. Sublingual nitroglycerin 0.4mg as needed for ischemic chest pain relief. Intravenous infusion of Metoprolol tartrate to achieve a target resting heart rate between 60 and 70 beats per minute. High-intensity statin therapy with Atorvastatin 80mg daily. Guideline-directed medical therapy initiation with Ramipril 2.5mg daily once hemodynamically stable, together with carvedilol or metoprolol to reduce myocardial oxygen demand and prevent adverse post-infarction left ventricular remodeling. Dietary & Lifestyle Advice: Strict cardiac diet restricting sodium intake to less than 2 grams per day, eliminating trans fats, limiting saturated fats, and emphasizing Mediterranean dietary patterns rich in leafy greens, fiber, and lean proteins. Patient is enrolled in the outpatient Phase II Cardiac Rehabilitation program starting three weeks post-discharge. Patient is advised that if any recurrence of retrosternal pressure, crushing chest pain, dyspnea at rest, presyncope, or palpitations lasting more than five minutes that do not respond to sublingual nitroglycerin occurs, they must call 911 or activate emergency medical transport immediately. Follow up date: Review after 2 weeks for driving and light sedentary desk work resumption if no recurrent angina or exertional dyspnea is present.
