## Plan of Care
Medications Prescribed:
- Sompraz-D Capsule (Esomeprazole 40 milligrams and Domperidone 30 milligrams sustained release): Take once daily, one capsule with a glass of water 30 to 45 minutes before breakfast for 28 days.
- Mucaine Gel (Oxetacaine 10 mg, Aluminium Hydroxide 291 mg and one hour after dinner, and immediately before bedtime for 14 days.
- Ferrous Ascorbate with Folic Acid (Tablet Orofer-XT) (Elemental Iron 100 milligrams and Folic acid 1.5 milligrams): Take twice a day, two hours after lunch or at 4 PM with orange juice. Do not take with tea or coffee. Avoid taking with Sompraz-D.
- Continue current medications: Tablet Glimepiride 1 mg once daily before breakfast; Tablet Metformin 500 mg twice daily after meals; Tablet Telmisartan 40 mg once daily in the morning at 10 AM; Tablet Atorvastatin 10 mg at night before sleeping.
Dietary & Lifestyle Advice:
- Adopt small, frequent meals (four to five times a day) instead of large meals to reduce gastric pressure.
- Avoid caffeine (tea, coffee), carbonated drinks, sour juices, excessive oil, fried foods, spices, chocolate, garlic, and raw onions as they relax the esophageal sphincter.
- Consume iron-rich non-heme iron sources like spinach, mustard greens, dried raisins, dates, green peas, beans, amaranth, and lentils. Mix a pinch of lemon juice with leafy greens to enhance iron absorption.
- Avoid taking calcium supplements or dairy products at the same time as iron tablets; maintain a minimum 3-hour interval between them. Take iron tablets only in the evening if consuming milk/dairy in the morning.
- Practice brisk walking for 30 to 40 minutes daily and perform ankle pump exercises while sitting on a chair every 45 minutes to improve venous return.
- Limit sleep time: Do not sleep immediately after dinner; wait at least 2 to 3 hours. Aim to finish dinner before 8 PM and sleep by 10 PM.
- Elevate the head-end of the bed by 6 inches (using a firm pillow or bricks) rather than using multiple pillows, as this helps prevent acid reflux.
- Sleep in the left lateral decubitus position (left side down).

Patient Education & Red Flag Warnings:
- Patient is instructed to seek immediate emergency evaluation if experiencing progressive dysphagia or odynophagia. He is also warned to report hematemesis or coffee-ground emesis, melena (black, foul-smelling stools), sudden severe chest pain radiating to the left arm or neck, or any difficulty breathing/severe dizziness.
