Dietary & Lifestyle Advice: Recommend a strict cardiac diet restricting sodium intake to less than 2 grams per day, eliminating trans fats, limiting saturated fats, and emphasizing Mediterranean dietary patterns rich in leafy greens, fiber, and lean proteins. Investigations & Procedures Ordered: Stat 12-lead ECG performed; Blood drawn for serial cardiac Troponin-I and metabolic panels; Continuous cardiac telemetry placed; Urgent cardiology consultation arranged for admission within 24 hours. Follow up Date: Enroll patient in outpatient Phase II Cardiac Rehabilitation program starting three weeks post-discharge. Patient can generally resume driving after one week and light sedentary desk work after two weeks, provided there is no recurrent angina or exertional dyspnea.
