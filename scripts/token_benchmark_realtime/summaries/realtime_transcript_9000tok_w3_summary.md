## Plan of Care

Dietary & Lifestyle Advice:
- Dietary modifications: Consume small, frequent meals (four to five times a day) instead of large meals. Avoid tea, coffee, sodas, carbonated drinks, sour juices on an empty stomach, excessive oil, fried snacks, spices, chocolate, garlic, and raw onions as these can relax the esophageal valve.
- Sleep and Posture: Consume dinner before 8 PM and aim to sleep by 10 PM. Avoid lying down immediately after eating; remain upright for at least two to three hours. Elevate the head-end of the bed by 6 inches (using a firm pillow or bricks) rather than using multiple pillows.
- Exercise: Engage in light brisk walking for 30 to 40 minutes daily, and perform simple ankle pump exercises while sitting on a chair every 45 minutes to prevent venous stasis.
- Red Flag Warnings: Patient is instructed to seek immediate emergency evaluation if there is progressive dysphagia or odynophagia, hematemesis or coffee-ground emesis, melena (black, foul-smelling stools), acute chest pain radiating to the left arm or neck with associated confusion, or severe shortness of breath and dizziness. Avoid purchasing over-the-counter pain killers like Ibuprofen, Diclofenac, Aceclofenac, Mefenamic acid; only Paracetamol 650 mg after meals should be taken for mild headache or body aches.
Follow up date: Review in four weeks with CBC, Fasting and Postprandial Blood Sugar, and Serum Ferritin. The Sompraz-D course will be discontinued by reducing the dose after four weeks if symptoms improve significantly. Endoscopy is not required at this time; it may be considered after six months if necessary.
