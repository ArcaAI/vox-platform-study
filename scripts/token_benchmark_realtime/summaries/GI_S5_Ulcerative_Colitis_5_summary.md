## Reports

## Current Diagnosis
Ulcerative colitis, left-sided, proctosigmoiditis, moderately active.

## Plan of Care
Mesacol 1.2 gram OD twice daily before food for the duration of treatment.
Mesacol suppository 1 gram at night.
Patient is advised to take the medicine every day, even when feeling better. Patient is instructed to come immediately if there is fever, more than eight stools a day, severe abdominal pain, or swelling of the abdomen. Follow up in four weeks with the report.
