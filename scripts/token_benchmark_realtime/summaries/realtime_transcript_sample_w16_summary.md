## Plan of Care
- Treatment: 1.
- Sompraz-D Capsule (Esomeprazole 40 mg and Domperidone 30 mg sustained release): Take once daily at 30 to 45 minutes before breakfast with a glass of water for 28 days continuously.
- Esomeprazole is a PPI to reduce acid production by over 90%.
- Domperidone increases gastric motility.
- 2.
- Mucaine Gel (10 ml): Take three times daily one hour after meals, and once before bedtime for 14 days.
- This will coat the esophageal lesions, provide immediate relief from burning sensation, and cause temporary numbness.
- 3.
- Ferrous Ascorbate with Folic Acid (Tablet Orofer-XT) (Elemental Iron 100 mg and Folic acid 1.5 mg): Take two hours after lunch or at 4 PM with orange juice to enhance iron absorption for 60 days.
- Vitamin C in the form of orange juice will significantly increase iron absorption.
- 4.
- Continue current medications: Tablet Glimepiride 1 mg before breakfast, Tablet Metformin 500 mg twice daily after meals, and Tablet Telmisartan 40 mg at 10 AM every morning.
- Atorvastatin 10 mg is taken at bedtime.
- Lifestyle Modifications: 1.
- Diet: Consume small, frequent meals instead of large ones (four to five times a day).
- Avoid caffeine, carbonated drinks, acidic orange juice on an empty stomach, excessive oil, fried foods, spices, chocolate, garlic, and raw onions as these can relax the esophageal sphincter.
- 2.
- Sleep and Positioning: Do not sleep for at least 2 to 3 hours after dinner; aim to sleep by 10 PM.
- Elevate the head-end of the bed by 6 inches using a firm pillow or bricks under the bed, do not use extra pillows as this can bend the neck.
- Lie in the left lateral decubitus position during sleep to help keep acid levels above the junction.
- Avoid tight belts and restrictive clothing.
- 3.
- Exercise: Practice light brisk walking for 30 to 40 minutes daily.
- Perform simple ankle pump exercises while sitting on a chair every 45 minutes to improve venous return.
- Pain Management: Only take Paracetamol 650 mg after meals for mild headache or fever; avoid NSAIDs like Ibuprofen, Diclofenac, Aceclofenac, and Mefenamic acid as they can cause severe ulcers and bleeding.
- Follow-up: Return in four weeks to this OP for Complete Blood Count (CBC) to check for improvement from Hemoglobin 11.2 g/dL, Fasting and Postprandial Blood Sugar levels, and Serum Ferritin level after two months.
