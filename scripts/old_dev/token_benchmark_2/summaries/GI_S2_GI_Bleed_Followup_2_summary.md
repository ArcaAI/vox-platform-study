## Current Diagnosis
Healing gastric ulcer, NSAID-related, post-haemorrhagic anaemia, Hypertension.

Patient advised to never take diclofenac or any similar painkiller again; can use paracetamol 650 mg up to three times a day for knee pain instead. Follow-up endoscopy and biopsy in eight weeks to check if the ulcer has healed. Follow-up Hb test in four weeks. Patient instructed to return immediately to casualty if black stools, vomiting, or dizziness occur. Patient advised to come back with the Hb report in four weeks.
