## Presenting Complaints
Patient presents with abdominal pain for three months. Pain is located below the chest and in the back, described as burning sensation. Pain increases when the stomach is full and sometimes wakes the patient at night due to pain. Patient reports relief with certain foods. Patient has a history of heartburn and bloating.

## Past History
Patient has been taking Gelusil syrup when pain occurs, and ibuprofen for back pain about two to three times a week. Patient has a history of allergy to clarithromycin from last year, which caused itching and hives throughout the body.

## Family History
Father had an ulcer.

## Drug History
Patient has taken Gelusil syrup when pain occurs and ibuprofen for back pain about two to three times a week. Patient has a known allergy to clarithromycin.

## Reports
Stool test from October 25th showed H. pylori stool antigen positive, with Hb of 13.2.

## Current Diagnosis
Dyspepsia due to H. pylori infection, possibly a peptic ulcer, secondary to NSAID use.

## Plan of Care
Patient will be prescribed Pantoprazole 40 mg twice daily in the morning and night before meals. Amoxicillin 1 gram twice a day. Levofloxacin 500 mg once a day for fourteen days. Patient instructed to stop ibuprofen completely and take paracetamol only for back pain. Patient instructed to stop smoking. Patient may continue taking Gelusil syrup intermittently. Patient advised to repeat stool H. pylori antigen test after two weeks of Pantoprazole. Patient is instructed to return with the report in eight weeks, or immediately to the casualty if there is vomiting blood or black stools.
