## Presenting Complaints
Patient presents with fever for five days. Associated symptoms include chills and body aches, headache, and pain behind the eyes. Patient reports one episode of vomiting yesterday.

## Past History
Patient has a history of typhoid fever in 2019, requiring a five-day admission at Kottayam medical college.

## Family History
Two people in the locality had dengue.

## Drug History
Paracetamol 650 mg three times daily for fever. Patient has a penicillin allergy due to a rash and hives after taking amoxicillin two years ago.

## Hospital Admissions
Admitted for typhoid fever in 2019 at Kottayam medical college for five days.

## General Examination & Vitals
Temperature 101.2, pulse 102, BP 110/70. Throat normal. No lymph nodes. No rash. Chest clear. Abdomen soft, no tenderness, liver not palpable.

## Previous Diagnosis
Acute febrile illness, probably dengue fever.

## Reports
Blood test from October 28th showed WBC 3,800 and platelets 1.4 lakh, noted as slightly low white count.

## Current Diagnosis
Dengue fever (suspected).

## Plan of Care
Investigations ordered include dengue NS1, dengue IgM, CBC, malaria smear, and urine routine tests. Patient advised to drink plenty of fluids such as water, ORS, or rice water, aiming for at least three liters. Paracetamol 650 mg may be given only if fever is present, not exceeding four tablets per day. Advised to avoid pain relievers like ibuprofen. Instructed patient to return with reports in two days. Patient instructed to come to the casualty immediately if bleeding, abdominal pain, recurrent vomiting, or dizziness occurs.
