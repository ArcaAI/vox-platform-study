## Plan of Care
Initiate treatment with a daycare drip of ferric carboxymaltose 1000 mg mixed with normal saline 100 ml over 20 minutes. Start folic acid 5 mg once daily after breakfast for 30 days. Patient instructed not to repurchase iron tablets. Follow up in two weeks with CBC, reticulocyte count, and endoscopy reports. Stool occult blood and tTG IgA tests have already been performed. Patient education provided: Avoid tea and coffee immediately after meals. Consume spinach, moringa leaves, dates, and legumes. Instructed to seek immediate care at the OPD if black stools, chest pain, or unbearable dizziness occur; otherwise, proceed to casualty and wait one hour before transport if symptoms like itching or shortness of breath arise during drip.
