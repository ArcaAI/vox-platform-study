The callback _make_frame_handler() feeds audio into StreamingPreprocessor through 6 synchronized stages:
1. Slicing 32ms Blocks
Slices continuous byte stream into discrete 512-sample (32ms at 16kHz) blocks normalized to [-1.0, 1.0].
2. Denoising RNNoise / DeepFilter
Strips clinic air conditioning / fan background hum and acoustic HVAC rumble.
3. Silero VAD prob > 0.60
Evaluates speech probability prob = is_speech(frame). When prob > 0.60, transitions to active speech and prepends a 320ms pre-speech ring buffer (so opening soft consonants like /p/, /t/ are not clipped).
4. Endpointing 700ms Silence
When silence persists for 700ms, cuts the sentence boundary and emits AudioUtterance(samples, is_final=True).
5. Whisper ASR Inference CTranslate2 • FP16
StreamingInferenceWorker transcribes the audio using CTranslate2 FP16 Faster-Whisper on GPU with initial_prompt context.
6. RepeatGuard & Lexicon Phonetic Snap
Collapses repetitive transformer hallucination loops and corrects clinical drug names using the phonetic medical dictionary.

1. IngestionConsumer
Redis Ingress
What It Manages & Stores

Persistence: Redis Consumer Group cursor.

State & Handles: AUDIO_CONSUMER_GROUP name, worker-specific consumer ID, _last_id cursor, XAUTOCLAIM timer (min idle 5000ms) for reclaiming orphaned audio on worker restart.

Core Responsibilities

Streams raw audio chunks from stt:audio:{session_id}. Guarantees at-least-once delivery; unpacks binary Redis hash fields into structured AudioFrame objects.

🔗 Open redis_streams.py:103-270
2. StreamingPreprocessor
DSP & Framing
What It Manages & Stores

Persistence: In-memory session buffers.

State & Handles: pcm_remainder byte buffer, _resample_carry context array for polyphase filter, _peak_window moving average, _normalizer_gain scalar, optional RNNoise denoiser handle.

Core Responsibilities

Ingests variable-length PCM chunks, applies anti-aliased polyphase resampling to 16kHz float32, performs peak normalization, and chunks samples into 512-sample (32ms) frames.

🔗 Open preprocessor.py:528-616
3. Silero VAD State Machine
Voice Activity
What It Manages & Stores

Persistence: ONNX session LSTM tensors in memory.

State & Handles: LSTM hidden state vectors (h, c) carried across frames, speech_onset_frames counter, pre_speech_ring buffer (~320ms audio), silence_frames countdown counter.

Core Responsibilities

Computes speech probability per 32ms frame. Evaluates onset threshold (prob > 0.6) and silence timeout (700ms hysteresis) to segment continuous audio into clean AudioUtterance objects.

🔗 Open preprocessor.py:617-750
4. StreamingInferenceWorker
ASR Engine
What It Manages & Stores

Persistence: In-memory GPU worker state.

State & Handles: _asr_pipeline CTranslate2 Whisper model handle, _queue asyncio buffer, _prev_text conditioning prompt window, cumulative_processing_seconds RTF counter.

Core Responsibilities

Runs batched GPU Whisper inference on segmented speech utterances. Employs beam search and conditioning prompts to transcribe speech into text tokens with timestamps.

5. Guard Filters & Lexicon
Post-Processing
What It Manages & Stores

Persistence: In-memory dictionary and regex rules.

State & Handles: RepeatGuard n-gram loop history window, LexiconCorrector radix tree of clinical hotwords, hallucination RMS energy thresholds, overlap boundary deduplication buffers.

Core Responsibilities

Prunes hallucinated Whisper repetitions, drops low-energy filler tokens, and snaps phonetic mistranscriptions to authoritative clinical medical vocabulary terms.
